GLOSSARY

AVL tree — A self-balancing binary search tree where heights of left and right subtrees differ by at most one for every node.
Amortized analysis — A method to average operation cost over a sequence rather than worst-case per operation.
Binary search tree — A tree where each node has at most two children and left keys are smaller than the parent key.
