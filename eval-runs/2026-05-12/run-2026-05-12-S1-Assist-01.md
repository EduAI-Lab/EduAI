## User turn 1

Explain what "gradient descent" means for someone new to machine learning, in one short paragraph of plain language (no math notation).

## Assistant reply 1

## Gradient Descent

**Top summary:**
- Gradient descent is how a machine learning model learns by fixing its own mistakes, step by step.
- It works by asking: "which small adjustment to my settings reduces my error the most?" - then making that adjustment, repeatedly.
- After thousands of tiny adjustments, the model lands on settings that produce accurate predictions.

---

## Step ladder

1. The model makes a prediction and compares it to the correct answer.
2. It measures how wrong it was - this is called the **loss** (the gap between predicted and actual).
3. It figures out which direction to nudge its internal settings to shrink that loss.
4. It takes one small step in that direction.
5. Repeat from step 1 until the loss is small enough.
