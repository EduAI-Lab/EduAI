## Assistant reply 1 (96 words)

### Top Summary
- Scrape, wash, rinse, dry — in that order
- Hot soapy water does most of the work
- Glasses first, pots last (cleanest to dirtiest)

### Step Ladder

1. **Scrape** leftover food into the bin; rinse off loose bits under the tap
2. **Fill** the sink with hot water and a squirt of dish soap
3. **Wash** items cleanest-to-dirtiest: glasses → plates → cutlery → pots
4. **Rinse** each item under clean running water to remove all soap
5. **Dry** with a clean towel, or air-dry on a rack
