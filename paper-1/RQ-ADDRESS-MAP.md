## Intro wording (drop into §1 when drafting)

> We organize the work around four questions from our Form A programme. **RQ1** motivates the ADHD Assist pillars (§3). **RQ2** asks whether those scaffolds survive multi-turn interaction (§5). **RQ3** — our primary research question — asks whether a second-pass Dean improves structural adherence over prompting alone (§4–§5). **RQ4** asks whether Assist reduces load and improves learning for ADHD learners in authentic use; here we report only a small protocol feasibility pilot with descriptive human metrics (§6), not a confirmatory efficacy test.
