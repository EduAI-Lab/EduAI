## Human pass — 2026-08-17 (Ahab)

Walked Core as a real user in a browser: typed email/password on `/auth/login` (not cookie injection), clicked through the UI, and checked whether each path made sense. Accounts: `student1@eduai.local` (Alex Patel), `student2@eduai.local` (Brooke Kim), `ta.cs@eduai.local` (Sam Carter). Password is the seed default.

What I actually clicked, and what I saw:

1. **Login / onboarding.** Both Student and TA land on "Link your UBC student number" after sign-in. The skip control is a real button: "I don't have a UBC student number — skip for now". Clicking it as Sam left `/onboarding/student-id` and opened `/dashboard`. Same skip works for Alex. This is the #1464 escape hatch, and it is usable, not just a cookie.
2. **Student dashboard.** After skip: "Good afternoon, Alex", "Your AI-powered learning companion", "Courses enrolled 6", course cards with Chat links. No "Courses assisting". Sidebar chip says "Student". Clear.
3. **TA dashboard.** "Good afternoon, Sam", "Your assigned courses and student activity", **Courses assisting 2**, COSC 101 / COSC 121. Sidebar chip still says "Student" (`ta.cs@eduai.local`) — same design tradeoff already in the table, not a new bug.
4. **Course list.** Student sees "Courses You Are Enrolled In" grouped by term with Enrolled tags (COSC 101, ENGL 110, MATH 200, PHYS 121, …). TA list after a denied redirect is "Courses You Are Assisting In".
5. **COSC 101 (own course).** Instructor card is **Dr. Ada Lovelace**, TA roster is **Sam Carter**. The old "No professor assigned" copy is gone (#1463).
6. **Unrelated course (HIST 210 / MATH 200).** Redirect to `/courses?access=denied` and a red banner: "You do not have access to that course. Open a course from this list only." Makes sense.
7. **Enrolled but unpublished (student2 / COSC 211).** Redirect to `/courses?access=unpublished` and banner: "That course isn't published yet. You'll be able to open it once your instructor publishes it." Makes sense — this is the #1465 fix, and a person can tell it apart from the denied banner.
8. **Course chat (`/chat`, not `/courses/:id/chat`).** History, New chat, composer, and the TA privacy notice ("Instructors, teaching assistants, and platform admins can view this course chat history") all render. I typed a real question and sent it. No assistant reply appeared. That matches #1510 / no reachable model — I am not marking guardrail/off-topic *content* as human-verified.
9. **`/help`.** Student correctly does not see Course materials or Administration. On the tree I walked, TA help also hid Course materials (Getting started / Command palette / Courses / AI chatbot only). That is the defect Stavan flagged. This PR's `help.tsx` Enrollment.role lookup is the fix; the e2e asserts `#materials` visible and `#administration` absent. I am not pretending I re-clicked Help on the post-fix bundle in this same sitting.
10. **`/settings`.** Account / Accessibility / Providers, password-change fields, API keys. Copy is understandable. I did not submit a live password change (would lock the seed account).

I did **not** personally click the API-only probes (question-bank 403s, enrollment IDOR, `canManageTopics` policy toggles, bug-report dialog submit). Those stay on the Playwright assertions added for the review, not on this walk.

Live-model guardrail content is still blocked on a reachable LLM. That remains a known gap, tracked by #1510, not a row I am willing to rubber-stamp.
