# Reviewer Response: ADHD Assist Mode and Communication Support

## Summary of the response

We appreciate the feedback and believe it strengthens the framing of this work in three important ways: clarifying the contribution, grounding the project in prior research, and making the evaluation strategy more persuasive. The central argument of this work is that ADHD Assist mode is not simply a visual interface change, but a content-level intervention designed to improve communication support for learners with attention-related difficulties.

## How we address the feedback

### 1. Grounding the work in prior research
We will strengthen the paper by situating the project within existing literature on ADHD, communication support, attention-related difficulties, and prior approaches to adaptive or assistive feedback. This will help show that the work builds on established concerns in the field rather than appearing as a disconnected technical novelty.

### 2. Justifying the evaluation approach
We will more clearly explain why the selected evaluation approach is appropriate for measuring the impact of the intervention. The goal is to show that the method is not arbitrary, but is the most suitable way to assess whether ADHD Assist mode meaningfully changes how learners receive and process support.

### 3. Strengthening the credibility of the results
We will frame the findings within the broader context of prior work so reviewers can better understand how the results compare to existing evidence. This will help establish both the credibility of the results and the significance of the contribution.

### 4. Clarifying the contribution of dynamic routing and multi-stage processing
We will make the contribution of the dynamic routing or multi-stage process more explicit. Rather than presenting this as an architecture-only idea, we will explain how the approach is used to improve response quality, adaptivity, and support for learners with attention challenges. We will also be careful to define the boundaries of the approach and avoid overstating what it can achieve.

### 5. Connecting the technical design to ADHD needs
A key point we will emphasize is that the architecture is meaningful because it is applied to a real user need: helping learners with ADHD receive communication that is more accessible, structured, and usable. The connection between the system design and the specific needs of ADHD learners will be made explicit throughout the paper.

### 6. Emphasizing learner impact
We will make the practical impact on learners more central to the narrative. The paper will explain not only how the system functions technically, but also how it may affect learner experience, comprehension, engagement, and support in real educational settings.

### 7. Positioning the paper for the right audience
We will revise the framing so that the paper clearly identifies where it fits in the broader research landscape. This includes explaining the intended community, the relevance of the work to that community, and how the contribution should be presented to maximize clarity and impact.

### 8. Aligning the paper with the publication venue
We will tailor the paper’s framing to match the expectations of the target venue and community. This includes being explicit about the contribution, the significance of the findings, and the relevance of the work to that audience.

### 9. Incorporating approved feedback
We will ensure that the approved feedback is integrated directly into the paper rather than treated as peripheral comments. This will help make the manuscript more coherent and better aligned with reviewer expectations.

### 10. Strengthening the statistical framing
We will also refine the evaluation and significance framing so that the statistical claims are more defensible and better aligned with standard expectations. In particular, we will aim for a p-value near 0.05 where appropriate and ensure that the analysis and interpretation are consistent with that target.

## Closing statement

Overall, the revision will emphasize that this work is not only a technical implementation, but a meaningful, learner-centered intervention grounded in prior research and designed to improve communication support for ADHD learners. The revised framing will make the contribution clearer, the evaluation more justified, and the impact more compelling.
